The Marketing Catalog: Unifying Campaigns Into Initiatives

Marketing was running dozens of campaigns at once across a dozen channels, and stakeholders wanted them viewed together as a single initiative — but nothing in the data logically tied a Facebook campaign to the email send and landing page that were actually part of the same push. Naming conventions alone couldn't fix that. So I designed and built the Marketing Catalog: a tool where marketers log every campaign and tie it to a shared Initiative. The catalog then mints a unique, trackable UTM Campaign value for each one — along with cleaned-up, standardized UTM Source and Medium values — so reporting can roll up by Initiative or drill into any single campaign, and every dollar of web and conversion data traces back to exactly where it came from.
